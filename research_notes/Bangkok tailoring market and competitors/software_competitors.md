# Software competitors for small tailor shops (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-10-26. About 24 searches and page fetches. Google Play pages did not render through the fetch tool, so Play ratings and review counts mostly could not be checked. Each fact below has its own source; anything unchecked is listed under Gaps.

## Which products exist, and what do they offer (owner, platforms, features, price, ratings, last update)?

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups. (1) Cheap phone "measurement book" apps, mostly made in India or Pakistan, many on Android. (2) Indian cloud ERPs sold per year, often at a custom quote (TailorPad, Fit4bond, Atelierware, TailorMate). (3) Western work-order SaaS at US$29-99 or more per month (Orderry, GarmentDesk, RepairDesk). (4) New iOS-native apps with almost no reviews (Tailored Made, TailorFlow, Stylo Tailor). No one product combines measurement history with "changes since last fitting", voice dictation, fabric deduction in metres, referral links and Thai on iOS.

### Cited Findings

**Western / international SaaS**
- **Orderry** (cloud, work orders). It is marketed to ateliers and tailors. Features: customer profiles with saved measurements and measurement history, fitting notes, due dates, order status stages, deposit tracking and balances, digital invoices, payment links by SMS/email, POS, inventory for materials including fabric, SMS/email "order ready" alerts, barcode tags, QuickBooks/Xero integration, iOS/Android apps. 7-day free trial, no card needed — [Orderry for atelier](https://orderry.com/for-atelier/)
- Orderry pricing: Hobby from $39/mo, Startup from $69/mo, Business from $99/mo, Enterprise from $199/mo, flat rate per month — [Capterra Orderry pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/151174/Orderry/pricing/)
- **GarmentDesk**: "Plans starting at just $29/month". 14-day free trial, no card. Covers bespoke, alteration and repair orders, CRM with measurements, invoices and payment tracking, multi-store, team access controls, automatic customer notifications, analytics. Aimed at the USA, UK, AU, NZ and EU. The tier breakdown was not on the homepage — [GarmentDesk](https://garmentdesk.com/)
- **RepairDesk** (repair-shop POS, also positioned for tailors): $79/store/mo billed annually, $99/store/mo billed monthly — [RepairDesk tailor page](https://www.repairdesk.co/tailor-shop-management-software/) (taken from the search snippet; the page itself was not fetched)
- **ThreadNix**: Free plan (up to 5 designs, 1 team member) up to Premium. Exact prices not checked — [ThreadNix](https://threadnix.com/) (search snippet)
- **Dress Measurement** (developer Aeeiee), iPhone and Android. Free tier: measurement profiles and brand size advice, no ads. "Tailor Shop" is **$12.99/month or $59.99/year** (7-day trial): unlimited clients, custom measurements, photos and notes, CSV export, orders, invoices, fitting schedule with reminders, client messaging (WhatsApp/SMS/email), fabric "via notes" only, cloud sync (Android). Measurement history is kept: "New entries add to the history. Nothing is overwritten" — [dressmeasurement.com](https://dressmeasurement.com/). A search snippet gives 4.4 stars from 1,154 ratings, but which store that refers to is unclear — [search result](https://dressmeasurement.com/)
- **Tailor Body Measurements by DM** (iOS): annual subscription about $2.92/mo with a 7-day trial — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailor-body-measurements-by-dm/id1125279242) (search snippet)
- **Tailored (thetailoredco)**: measures garments, not bodies, from a photo. Native iOS/Android apps since Feb 2025, from $7/mo, 14-day trial — [Tailored](https://www.thetailoredco.com/tailored-for-ios-and-android/) (search snippet)

**New iOS-native shop apps (very few reviews)**

Features: measurements, **fabric inventory with low-stock alerts**, worker assignment, orders, invoices, role-based multi-user access. Rated **1.0 from 1 rating**; the review says "It asks for a login but there is no way to create an account" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailorflow/id6741868343)
- **Stylo Tailor**, by Nokofio Platforms Ltd. Free, English, no ratings yet, actively updated (v1.3.62). Aimed at African fashion businesses. Features: measurements, fabric photos, job stages Pending → In Progress → Completed → Delivered, apprentice tasks, messaging, portfolio, invoices, mobile-money payments, insights — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stylo-tailor/id6755918222)

### Inferences
- Most products with real traction are Android-first or web ERPs from South Asia, or US/EU work-order SaaS at $29-199/mo. The iOS-native shop apps have 0-7 ratings each, so the iOS slot is not crowded but demand there is unproven.
- Orderry is the most complete feature match: measurement history, stages, deposits, fabric inventory, SMS, mobile apps. But it starts at $39/mo (about 4x Pro) and is not tailor-specific in its UX.
- Dress Measurement's $12.99/mo Tailor Shop tier sits closest to the planned $9.99 Pro in price and scope, and it already offers fitting reminders and WhatsApp messaging.

## Which handle fabric inventory, measurement history, deposits/receipts, referrals or reviews? Which are cloud/multi-device?

### Takeaway
Fabric inventory appears in Orderry, TailorFlow, TailorPad and Atelierware (barcode, advanced tier). None found tracks metres or deducts at cutting. Measurement history is explicit only in Orderry, Atelierware and Dress Measurement. Deposits are explicit only in Orderry. **No tailor product found has a referral programme or Google-review requests.**

### Cited Findings
- Orderry: measurement history, deposit and balance tracking, invoices, inventory including fabric, SMS/email — [Orderry](https://orderry.com/for-atelier/)
- TailorFlow: fabric inventory with low-stock alerts, role-based multi-user — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailorflow/id6741868343)
- Dress Measurement: history is never overwritten; fabric only "via notes"; cloud sync noted for Android — [dressmeasurement.com](https://dressmeasurement.com/)
- Atelierware: measurements and history, WhatsApp reminders, reports; barcode inventory in advanced plans — [Atelierware](https://www.atelierware.com/)
- Tailored Made: QR receipts, PayPal links, cloud sync across devices — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailored-made-measure-deliver/id6445846228)
- TailorMate: cloud storage, so data survives losing the phone — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/tailormate-app)
- Tailor App by SK Business Logics: printable barcode receipts, customer history — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/tailor-app-by-sk-business-logics/tailor-app-by-sk-business-logics)
- Thai Windows "Tailor Shop" program: local backup and restore only (single PC) — [Thaiware](https://www.thaiware.com/software/11326/)

### Inferences
- Features found in none of the sources checked: voice-dictated measurements (EN/TH), "changes since last fitting" diff, metre-based fabric deduction at cutting, referral links for LINE/WhatsApp, Google-review requests, monthly PDF report. These look like gaps in the market. That conclusion rests on the listings above, not on hands-on testing.
- Many cheap Android measurement-book apps are probably single-device with local storage. Not checked per app.

### Gaps
- Whether any app offers review requests or referrals could not be fully excluded, because Play listings and several named products were not checked.

## Are there Thai-made or Thai-language tailor/boutique management apps?

### Takeaway
The only Thai-made tailor software found is an old Windows desktop program (last updated 2017). The Thai cloud products found serve dress-rental shops, not tailors. One iOS tailor app, Tailored Made, offers a Thai interface.

### Cited Findings
- **Tailor Shop (โปรแกรมร้านตัดเสื้อ)** by NateePutchakeaw. Windows, v5.0, **last updated 7 Dec 2017 (abandoned)**, shareware **4,990 THB** (trial limited to 10 customers). Thai/English. Customer measurements (chest, shoulder, length, sleeve), order forms and receipts, history, backup. 4.88/5 from 74 votes, 3,376 downloads — [Thaiware](https://www.thaiware.com/software/11326/). A Thaiware shop listing gives the price as 5,900 baht — [Thaiware shop](https://shop.thaiware.com/4192-Tailor-Shop.html) (snippet; the two prices conflict)
- **RentSabai** (Thai, cloud). For wedding-dress rental shops and studios, not tailoring. **Basic ฿5,000/year, Premium ฿7,500/year**, 3 months free. Barcode inventory, Google Calendar sync, dashboard, AI receipt scanning, LINE OA support. Claims 500+ Thai shops — [RentSabai](https://rentsabai.com/)
- **BKRent**: Thai dress-rental POS/stock program — [BKRent](https://www.bkrent.com/product/41/rental-shop-system-evening-dress-rental-program)
- **POSPOS**: Thai POS with a guide for clothing shops (retail, not made-to-measure) — [POSPOS](https://www.pospos.co/article/detail/pospos-for-clothes-shop)
- **Tailored Made** includes Thai among its 10 languages — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailored-made-measure-deliver/id6445846228)

### Inferences
- Thai vendors show willingness to pay of roughly ฿5,000-7,500 per year (about US$12-18/mo) for cloud shop tools, with LINE support expected. That suggests US$9.99/mo Pro is reasonably priced for Thai SMEs, and $24.99 Premium is at the high end.
- Thai tailors who use software probably rely on generic tools (LINE, notebooks, POS). No source was found that confirms this.

### Gaps
- A store search for "ร้านตัดเสื้อ" / "ช่างตัดเสื้อ" on the App Store and Google Play could not be done directly. Thai mobile tailor apps may exist but did not appear in web search.

## Body-scan / AI measurement apps relevant to tailors, with pricing

### Takeaway
3DLOOK Mobile Tailor is the main tool for tailors and starts at $499/month, far beyond a small Bangkok shop. Other scanners were not priced.

### Cited Findings
- 3DLOOK Mobile Tailor 2.0: 80+ measurements and a 3D model from 2 smartphone photos in about 30 s; an embeddable widget or links sent by email/SMS; API on some plans. **From $499/month** — [3DLOOK pricing](https://3dlook.ai/pricing/) (snippet); [3DLOOK made-to-measure](https://3dlook.ai/mobile-tailor/for-made-to-measure/). Claims remakes cut by up to 90% and alterations by up to 50% (vendor claim) — [Mobile Marketing Magazine](https://mobilemarketingmagazine.com/3dlook-launches-mobile-tailor-2-0-body-measurement-and-3d-modelling-solution/)
- MTailor: a direct-to-consumer custom-shirt brand that uses phone measurement (launch coverage: shirts for $69) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1029755). It is not a tool licensed to tailors.

### Gaps
- Bodygram, Size Stream and MTailor licensing prices were not found.

## Typical price points and whether freemium is common

### Takeaway
Prices fall into three bands. Mobile-first apps cost US$3-13/mo, usually with a free tier. Indian cloud ERPs cost about ₹200-1,000/mo or US$480 and up per year. Western work-order SaaS costs US$29-199/mo with a trial, not a free tier. Freemium is common among the mobile apps.

### Cited Findings
- Free tier plus paid: Dress Measurement (free, then $12.99/mo or $59.99/yr) — [link](https://dressmeasurement.com/); Tailored Made (free, then $2.99/mo or $29.99/yr) — [link](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tailored-made-measure-deliver/id6445846228); ThreadNix free plan — [link](https://threadnix.com/); Setmore free — [link](https://www.zoho.com/bookings/explore/setmore-pricing.html); Fashion Dots from $0 — [link](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/fashion-dot-tailoring)
- Trial only: Orderry 7 days, $39-199/mo — [link](https://www.capterra.com/p/151174/Orderry/pricing/); GarmentDesk 14 days, from $29/mo — [link](https://garmentdesk.com/)
- Annual licence or quote: TailorPad $480-5,760/yr — [link](https://techjockey.com/detail/tailorpad); Fit4bond custom quote — [link](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/fit4bond); Atelierware ₹500/mo billed annually — [link](https://www.atelierware.com/)

### Inferences
- The planned Free (3 clients) / $9.99 / $24.99 is in line with the mobile-app band. $24.99 overlaps the bottom of the Western SaaS band ($29) and is about 2x Dress Measurement's top tier, so Premium needs clearly better features (multi-staff, reports, referrals) to justify it.
- A 3-client free cap is tighter than competitors (Tailored Made and Dress Measurement allow a usable free tier). It may affect conversion; no data was found to test this.

### Gaps
- No public data was found on conversion rates or how many users these apps have, beyond install counts.
