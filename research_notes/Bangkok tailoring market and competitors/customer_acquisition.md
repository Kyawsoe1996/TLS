# Bangkok tailors: how they win, keep and lose customers (2024-2026)

Researched 2026-10-26. About 20 searches and fetches. Google Maps pages could not be fetched (no live review counts captured). Several "best tailor" sources are written by tailor shops about themselves or are sponsored, and are marked as such below.

## 1. Acquisition channels (Google/TripAdvisor, touts, concierges, walk-ins, social, trunk shows)

### Takeaway
Two separate markets exist side by side. One is the tourist "package" trade, which gets customers from touts, tuk-tuk drivers and commission steering. The other is the reputable trade, which gets them from search (Google, TripAdvisor, listicles, sponsored articles), hotel recommendations, word of mouth, diplomatic/expat clienteles and trunk shows abroad. No source gives a reliable share of business by channel.

### Cited Findings
- Tuk-tuk/taxi commission steering, fake "government tailor sale" routines and Sukhumvit touting are described as "institutional", with commissions said to add 30-50% to the price. This comes from a Hoi An competitor's comparison page ("prices verified June 2026"), so it is a biased source — [Nathan Tailors, Bangkok vs Hong Kong](https://www.nathantailors.com/en/tailoring/bangkok-vs-hong-kong)
- The tuk-tuk/tailor-shop scam "has been running for decades". The driver earns commission for each shop stop. Advice: research tailors online first or ask your hotel — [Thailand Insider Guide, 2026 scams guide](https://thailandinsiderguide.com/?p=4522); [TakeMeTour blog](https://blog.takemetour.com/6-common-scams-in-bangkok-to-avoid/)
- The hotel concierge is the recommendation route travel guides suggest — [TakeMeTour](https://blog.takemetour.com/6-common-scams-in-bangkok-to-avoid/)
- Several Indian-run suit shops on Sukhumvit between Asoke and Nana "tout impressive clientele including US Secret Service agents and other government officials". Tailor on Ten (Soi 8) is described as avoiding "the tout-driven hustle of the tourist strips" — [Expique, Best of Bangkok's tailors](https://www.expique.com/best-bangkoks-tailors/) (undated; probably older)
- Rajawongse is known for a clientele of US diplomats and presidents. It also sells an Airbnb "Experience" ("Dress like a president with a renowned tailor"), rated 5.0 from 7-9 reviews as shown in search snippets on 2026-10-26 — [Hotels.com guide](https://www.hotels.com/go/thailand/great-bangkok-tailors); [Airbnb experience 6326142](https://www.airbnb.com/experiences/6326142)
- Trunk shows abroad: Tailor on Ten ran RSVP-booked trunk shows in Singapore, Sweden, Norway, Germany, the UK, Denmark and Australia. Orders are taken on iPad, and measurements and designs are saved to the customer's online account for later reorders. The page is dated 2016, so it is older — [Tailor on Ten trunk show page](https://www.tailoronten.com/pages/trunk-show-3)
- Alex Fashions (Amari Watergate) sent a sales manager to Australian cities every quarter with fabric samples (Australian Frequent Flyer forum, undated, probably older than 2020) — [AFF thread](https://www.australianfrequentflyer.com.au/community/threads/good-tailor-in-bangkok.27762/latest)
- Sponsored/paid editorial is a channel in its own right. Khaosod English ran a sponsored piece, "The Best tailor in Bangkok – Perfect tailor BKK" (2026-05-22). The Thaiger ran a "best of" profile of Woolrich Bespoke Tailor (2026-08-01) that reads as promotional — [Khaosod English sponsored](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/sponsored/2026/05/22/the-best-tailor-in-bangkok-perfect-tailor-bkk/); [Thaiger, Woolrich](https://thethaiger.com/guides/best-of/lifestyle/woolrich-bespoke-tailor-bangkok)
- Influencer and blogger deals: Singapore blog Wah So Shiok reviewed Narry with an exclusive promo code (WAHSOSHIOK = free shirt with every suit) (2024-07-14) — [Wah So Shiok](https://www.wahsoshiok.com/narry-bespoke-tailors-bangkok-review-promo-code-included/)
- Corporate/group channel: Woolrich serves "international corporate groups" with bulk orders (minimum 20 suits and 30 shirts per overseas trip) — [Thaiger, 2026-08-01](https://thethaiger.com/guides/best-of/lifestyle/woolrich-bespoke-tailor-bangkok)

### Inferences
- For a reputable shop, the visible acquisition levers are search and reviews, articles and influencers, and repeat customers or word of mouth. Touts are the reputational enemy. A review-request feature and a referral feature both strengthen the "reputable" channels.
- The usual "free shirt with a suit" promo-code offer is the industry's default incentive currency (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No data found on share of business by channel, or on concierge commission rates specifically. The 30-50% tout figure comes from one biased competitor page.
- Found nothing on Xiaohongshu, TikTok or Instagram use by Bangkok tailors, or on Chinese-tourist targeting. Reddit threads did not show up in search results.

## 2. Importance of Google reviews, ratings of known shops, review manipulation

### Takeaway
Ratings are near-uniformly high (4.4-5.0), so the rating barely tells shops apart. Review volume and recency matter more. The SEO/listicle space is visibly gamed: shops publish "best tailors in Bangkok" lists that rank themselves first.

### Cited Findings
- Narry Tailors on TripAdvisor: 4.9 bubbles, 615 reviews, #46 of 1,196 Bangkok shopping attractions (from search-result snippet, observed 2026-10-26; page not opened). Third-party listicles give Narry's Google rating variously as 4.9, 4.8 ("360+ reviews") and 4.5, so the figures conflict — [TripAdvisor Narry](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293916-d2523915-Reviews-Narry_Tailors-Bangkok.html); [besttailorbangkok.com](https://besttailorbangkok.com/best-tailors-in-bangkok/)
- A self-ranking listicle (updated 2026-10-16) is published by Signature's Collection, which ranks itself #1 and says so. It lists Google ratings without counts: Signature's 4.9, Rajawongse 4.5, Jesse & Son 4.6, Universal 4.7, Louis Collection 4.6, James Fashion 4.4, Unique Tailor 5.0, Galaxy 4.8, Narry 4.5, Empire 4.5. Suit prices run roughly 7,000-40,000+ THB — [besttailorbangkok.com](https://besttailorbangkok.com/best-tailors-in-bangkok/)
- Other shop-owned "best tailors 2026" lists: New Armani ("Top 10 Best Tailors in Bangkok 2026") and Unique Tailor ("7 Best Tailors in Bangkok for Suits (2026)") — [newarmanibkk.com](https://newarmanibkk.com/top-10-best-tailors-in-bangkok-2026/); [uniquetailorbangkok.com](https://uniquetailorbangkok.com/7-best-tailors-in-bangkok/)
- Shops use Google Business Profile microsites as landing pages, for example Overseas Tailor Silom 2nd branch — [business.google.com listing](https://business.google.com/v/overseas-tailor-silom-2nd-branch-bespoke-tailor-bangkok/07729896374990782896/1032/_/lp/1336715729766002203/)
- General evidence of Google review manipulation (UK): paid review networks post fake 5-star reviews on Google listings, with gaps in Google's monitoring (Which?, 2021, older, not tailor-specific) — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/news/2021/03/fake-google-reviews-boosting-uk-businesses/)
- Thailand-adjacent: Phuket real-estate agents fighting fake-review attacks (Phuket News, 2025) — [Phuket News](https://mail.thephuketnews.com/punked-phuket-real-estate-agents-fight-fake-reviews-attack-98199.php)

### Inferences
- With every shop at about 4.5-5.0, a steady flow of fresh, genuine reviews (and photos) after delivery is the competitive edge. That supports a post-delivery Google-review request. The request must not gate or incentivise reviews (against Google policy), especially in a market already suspected of manipulation.

### Gaps
- Could not record live Google Maps review counts for Rajawongse, Tailor on Ten, Universal, Pinky, Dynasty, Raja's, Marco, Empire, Tom's, Jesse, Ambassador & Smart, Nickermann's or Dusit (Maps not fetchable). These need checking by hand in a browser.
- No direct evidence on how Bangkok shops ask for reviews (cards, QR, WhatsApp follow-up), and no documented Bangkok-specific fake-review case.

## 3. Referral and loyalty practices

### Takeaway
No Bangkok shop's formal refer-a-friend programme was found. The prevailing incentive is a "free shirt with a suit" or a % discount handed out through promo codes, bloggers and hotels. Formal two-sided referral programmes exist at online made-to-measure brands (Indochino). Academic evidence says referred customers are worth more.

### Cited Findings
- Narry: promo code gives a free shirt with every suit (2024-07-14) — [Wah So Shiok](https://www.wahsoshiok.com/narry-bespoke-tailors-bangkok-review-promo-code-included/)
- Indochino (online made-to-measure) runs a store-credit refer-a-friend programme — [Referral Rock, Indochino example](https://referralrock.com/rms/program-example/indochino/); 16 menswear referral examples — [CustomerThink](https://customerthink.com/refer-a-friend-promotions-16-menswear-referral-marketing-examples/)
- Wharton/Goethe study (Schmitt, Skiera, Van den Bulte, 2011; older): about 10,000 customers of a German bank. Referred customers had higher margins and retention, and lifetime value at least 16% (16-25%) higher — [PDF](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/Schmitt-Skiera-vandenBulte-2011-Referral-Programs-Customer-Value.pdf)
- A vendor-compiled stat says referred customers show "37% higher retention" (attributed to Deloitte; secondary) — [Referral Factory stats](https://referral-factory.com/referral-marketing-statistics)

### Inferences
- A two-sided reward framed as "free shirt for both" matches local custom better than cash. Since tourists buy rarely, the referred friend is often a travelling colleague or a later visitor, so shareable links that work via WhatsApp/LINE matter.

### Gaps
- No reported results (uptake, conversion) for any tailor referral programme in Bangkok, Hoi An, Hong Kong, India or London.

## 4. Repeat business: measurements on file and reorders from abroad

### Takeaway
Keeping measurements on file and taking reorders through WhatsApp, email or online accounts is standard practice for reputable shops and a selling point. Competitors say package shops are often unreachable afterwards. Long-term loyalty shows up in forum anecdotes (10-12 years with one shop).

### Cited Findings
- Woolrich stores measurements and order history in client profiles. Reorders go through WhatsApp from anywhere (video measurement guidance), with worldwide shipping (2026-08-01) — [Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/guides/best-of/lifestyle/woolrich-bespoke-tailor-bangkok)
- Tailor on Ten: measurements and designs are saved to the customer's account for one-click reorders. Garments take 4-6 weeks to make plus 3-14 working days shipping. Shirts have a 3-piece minimum (older page) — [Tailor on Ten](https://www.tailoronten.com/pages/trunk-show-3)
- Reordering after the visit is "hit-or-miss": named shops answer email/WhatsApp, but package shops are unreachable (biased competitor, June 2026) — [Nathan Tailors](https://www.nathantailors.com/en/tailoring/bangkok-vs-hong-kong)
- A forum user had stayed with Tom's International Collection for 10-12 years. Rajawongse was praised for "transparent" pricing (undated, older) — [AFF](https://www.australianfrequentflyer.com.au/community/threads/good-tailor-in-bangkok.27762/latest)

### Inferences
- Measurement history, reminders and remote-reorder support are core to retention in this market. Receipts with the measurement and fabric record also act as proof that measurements are "remembered".

### Gaps
- No data on how often customers come back, repeat-customer share, or whether shops use newsletters or LINE broadcasts.

## 5. Reputation problems and trust builders

### Takeaway
The scam pattern is well documented: touts, "export company"/"government sale" pitches, full upfront payment, polyester in place of the promised wool or cashmere, shops that vanish, and 24-hour suits. Customers are told to pay only a deposit, get the price in writing, keep fabric samples and attend fittings. Those are the same things an app's receipts and fitting schedule formalise.

### Cited Findings
- The scam pattern: a friendly stranger offers tailoring cheaper than department stores and claims to be "an international export company that just opened". After upfront payment the customer gets an ill-fitting polyester garment instead of cashmere. Scammers are not always Thai (cases involving Singaporean, Burmese and Nepali operators). Sources are 2007-2008 plus a 2024 Reddit post — [Wikipedia, Thai tailor scam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_tailor_scam)
- Shops ask for large upfront sums. On return the shop may be closed or the suit not as promised. Advice: never pay in full upfront and write down the agreed price — [Thailand Insider Guide 2026](https://thailandinsiderguide.com/?p=4522); [TakeMeTour](https://blog.takemetour.com/6-common-scams-in-bangkok-to-avoid/)
- Forum advice: attend fittings, don't pay in full upfront, take fabric samples to compare at the first fitting — [AFF](https://www.australianfrequentflyer.com.au/community/threads/good-tailor-in-bangkok.27762/latest)
- Genuine half/full-canvas suits cost $400-900, against $150-350 for fused package-deal suits (June 2026, biased source) — [Nathan Tailors](https://www.nathantailors.com/en/tailoring/bangkok-vs-hong-kong)
- Permanent Style warns that Bangkok tailors making garments in 5 days are not necessarily good (from search snippet; page 403'd) — [Permanent Style](https://www.permanentstyle.com/brand/sam-the-tailor)
- New Armani Tailor Studio is praised for "honest and generous follow-up service when something does go wrong" — [Expique](https://www.expique.com/best-bangkoks-tailors/)
- Even reputable shops sell speed: Narry 3-4 days with a basted fitting the next day (2024) — [Wah So Shiok](https://www.wahsoshiok.com/narry-bespoke-tailors-bangkok-review-promo-code-included/). Woolrich express takes 24-48 h (2026) — [Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/guides/best-of/lifestyle/woolrich-bespoke-tailor-bangkok)

### Inferences
- Deposit receipts with the fabric named, a clear fitting/pickup timeline, and progress photos directly counter each step of the scam. They are worth marketing as "trust" features.

### Gaps
- No systematic complaint analysis (e.g. coded TripAdvisor 1-star reviews) found.

## 6. Seasonality

### Takeaway
Tourist demand fell in 2025: arrivals were down about 7% and Chinese arrivals down about 30%. Quiet periods are therefore a real concern for tourist-facing shops. No tailor-specific seasonality data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Thailand had 32.9 m foreign arrivals in 2025 (-7.2% vs 2024), its first non-pandemic fall in over a decade. Chinese arrivals fell about 30% to 4.47 m (Malaysia 4.52 m was #1). Causes cited: a strong baht (+17%), the Wang Xing abduction (Jan 2025), the Myanmar earthquake, the Cambodia border dispute and floods — [Thailand Business News](https://www.thailand-business-news.com/tourism/238071-thailand-sees-6-56-decline-in-foreign-tourist-arrivals-in-2025-china-remains-top-source); [Skift, 2026-01-20](https://dlr.skift.com/2026/01/20/thai-tourism-is-20-below-its-2019-peak/); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40055890)
- Sources give the 2025 decline as both 6.56% and 7.2% (they conflict) — same links

### Inferences
- Falling Chinese and East Asian walk-in traffic makes retaining expats, locals and remote reorder clients more valuable. That supports reminders and the monthly report.

### Gaps
- No evidence on how tailors handle peaks (Nov-Feb, CNY, Songkran, wedding season) or quiet months.

## 7. Business size data (AOV, orders/month, client base) for judging a "free up to 3 clients" tier

### Takeaway
Only price points are available. Suits cost roughly 7,000-40,000+ THB and shirts from about 1,200 THB. No source gives orders per month or active-client counts. But a working Bangkok shop has review counts in the hundreds (Narry 615 on TripAdvisor alone) and corporate orders of 20+ suits, which suggests even a small tailor deals with far more than 3 clients a month.

### Cited Findings
- Narry suits from 8,800 THB, shirts from 1,200 THB; reviewed suit 18,000 THB (2024) — [Wah So Shiok](https://www.wahsoshiok.com/narry-bespoke-tailors-bangkok-review-promo-code-included/)
- Suit price ranges across 10 shops: about 7,000 to 40,000+ THB (2026-10-16, self-interested publisher) — [besttailorbangkok.com](https://besttailorbangkok.com/best-tailors-in-bangkok/)
- 20,000 THB for 2 suits plus a tuxedo at Excelsior; 4,000-5,000 THB "over 10-12 years" at Tom's (older forum) — [AFF](https://www.australianfrequentflyer.com.au/community/threads/good-tailor-in-bangkok.27762/latest)
- Woolrich: free door-to-door delivery for orders of 10,000 THB or more; corporate minimum 20 suits and 30 shirts — [Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/guides/best-of/lifestyle/woolrich-bespoke-tailor-bangkok)
- Narry: 615 TripAdvisor reviews (snippet, 2026-10-26) — [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g293916-d2523915-Reviews-Narry_Tailors-Bangkok.html)

### Inferences
- A 3-client free tier works as a trial (try the app with a few real customers) but not as a usable plan for any operating shop. Most shops will hit the limit within days, which is the point of a conversion gate. It may feel stingy compared with free tiers based on orders per month.

### Gaps
- No data on average orders per shop per month, repeat-customer share, or active client-base size for small Bangkok tailors. This would need primary research (interviews with shop owners).
