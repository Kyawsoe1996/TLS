# Bangkok Tailoring / Bespoke Garment Market (2025–2026)

Currency note: the conversions below use roughly 1 USD = 32–33 THB (2025–26). The revenue figure from Khaosod/Ministry gives 1.53 trillion THB = US$48.8bn, which works out to about 31.4 THB/USD.

## 1. How many tailor shops exist in Bangkok and Thailand, and where are they?

### Takeaway
I found no official registry count of tailor shops in Bangkok. The figures that exist are old and anecdotal: about 5,000 "documented" tailoring businesses in Bangkok (2015 opinion piece) and about 500 Sikh-run tailor shops nationwide (source not verified). The tourist bespoke trade is concentrated on Sukhumvit between Nana and Asok, with further clusters in Silom/Bang Rak, Siam (MBK), Banglamphu/Phra Nakhon and Thonglor.

### Cited Findings
- "There must be, without exaggerating, about 5000 documented tailoring businesses in Thailand's capital" (Parisian Gentleman, 16 Sep 2015). This is OLD and an opinion estimate, not a registry figure — [Parisian Gentleman](https://www.parisiangentleman.com/blog/q-a-with-pg-editors-should-i-buy-a-suit-from-a-bangkok-tailor)
- Search snippets (they appear to come from Sikh community or older NBC coverage) say "many tailor shops in Bangkok are run by Sikhs, particularly in Sukhumvit, Silom and Banglamphu. Altogether, Sikhs run about 500 tailor shops in Thailand." I could not open the primary page (NBC returned 502), and Wikipedia's Sikhism in Thailand article does NOT contain this number. Treat it as unverified and probably dated (2009-era) — [NBC News (unfetched)](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna32541316); [Wikipedia: Sikhism in Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhism_in_Thailand)
- Wikipedia puts Thailand's Sikh community at about 70,000–100,000. It notes that Sikhs in the Pahurat area of Bangkok are mainly in textiles and real estate, and that Sikh businessmen moved to Phuket for tailoring and hotels — [Wikipedia: Sikhism in Thailand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sikhism_in_Thailand)
- Phahurat ("Little India", next to Chinatown) grew in the late 19th and early 20th centuries as a Punjabi/Sikh textile-trading district. This is the historical fabric supply base — [Wikipedia: Phahurat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phahurat)
- "Plenty of Indian suit shops on Sukhumvit Road, primarily concentrated between Asoke and Nana, including Rajawongse, Rajas and Gulati" (forum thread, via search snippet) — [Superfuture forum](https://supertalk.superfuture.com/topic/120967-best-tailor-in-bangkok/)
- Time Out Bangkok's list of the best tailor shops (dated 7 Aug 2026) shows where shops sit:
  - Sukhumvit Soi 11 (Jhasper Fashion), Soi 45 (Perfect Tailor BKK), Soi 10 (Jesse & Son), Soi 8 (Tailor on Ten), and near Nana (New Armani Tailor Studio)
  - Thonglor (Class Bespoke)
  - Silom Soi 14 (Woolrich Bespoke)
  - Royal Orchid Sheraton / Charoenkrung (Om Fashion)
  - MBK Center, Siam (Michael Tailor)
  - Phra Athit / Samsen Road, Phra Nakhon, near Khao San (Tailored By Luis)
  — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- Touting is visible: "stepping off the BTS near downtown Sukhumvit results in frequent sales pitches" — [Time Out Bangkok (search snippet)](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)

### Inferences
- The tourist bespoke belt (Sukhumvit Soi 3–45, Silom, Siam/MBK, Khao San/Banglamphu) probably holds a few hundred storefronts. The "5,000" figure, if it means anything, has to include Thai-local alteration shops, seamstresses and uniform makers across the whole city.
- The Thai-local layer (neighbourhood alteration stalls, market seamstresses, ห้องเสื้อ dressmakers) is far larger by count than the tourist layer, but it barely shows up in English-language sources.

### Gaps
- No Department of Business Development (DBD) count of registered companies under the TSIC tailoring codes (14xx clothing manufacture, or 9529 repair) for Bangkok. Most small shops are sole proprietors and are not registered as companies at all.
- No Google Maps or Wongnai shop-count scrape was available. A manual count of "tailor" and "ร้านตัดเสื้อ" in Bangkok would fill this gap.
- I found no data on department-store tailor counters (Central, Emporium, EmQuartier). One recruiter listing mentions a "tailor based in EmQuartier store" ([RGF](https://www.rgf-hragent.asia/en/thailand/tailor-based-in-emquartier-store-SDG-92485)), but I did not verify it.

## 2. Segments and price ranges (THB)

### Takeaway
There are five segments. Prices rise with each one: tourist 24–48h suits (about 6,000–10,000 THB), mid-range made-to-measure (15,000–30,000 THB), true bespoke (30,000–70,000+ THB), Thai traditional and dressmaker work (about 1,300–5,000 THB per outfit), and alterations (60–100 THB per simple job).

### Cited Findings
- Price tiers from SUITCUBE, a Bangkok tailor:
  - Budget made-to-measure or ready-to-wear with alterations: 8,000–15,000 THB, blends or microfibre
  - Mid-range: 15,000–30,000 THB, 100% wool, half-canvas
  - Bespoke: 30,000–70,000 THB, Super 120s+ or cashmere, full canvas
  - SUITCUBE's own prices: ready-to-wear suits from 3,900 THB, tailor-made from 6,600 THB, shirts from 990 THB
  - Turnaround: 5–7 days for ready-to-wear, 2–4 weeks for made-to-measure with 1–2 fittings
  — [SUITCUBE](https://www.suitcube.com/en/how-much-does-a-tailored-suit-cost-in-thailand/)
- Time Out's 2026 list gives these starting prices:
  - Om Fashion: suits from 6,000 THB, 2–3 days (express 12–24h)
  - Perfect Tailor BKK: from 8,000 THB, 24h on selected items
  - Michael Tailor (MBK): from 7,000 THB, 1–3 days
  - Woolrich: "8 hours to standard" turnaround
  - Tailor on Ten: premium tier, appointment-only, European fabrics
  - Tailored By Luis: value end
  — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- Sukhumvit Indian shops sell suits and shirts in 24–48h, at roughly US$200–400 (about 6,500–13,000 THB) for fused jackets (forum, via snippet) — [Superfuture forum](https://supertalk.superfuture.com/topic/120967-best-tailor-in-bangkok/)
- A 2026 press release says custom suits are finished in 2–3 days for US$250–750 (about 8,000–24,000 THB) — [AccessNewswire (snippet)](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/consumer-and-retail-products/custom-suits-in-2-days-make-bespoke-possible-on-bangkok-vacation-1190833)
- High-end bespoke in 2015 (OLD): Narin Couture charged about 22,000 THB (about US$700) for cut-make-trim on the customer's own fabric, with at least 2 weeks and 2–3 fittings. Parisian Gentleman endorsed only Narin and Tanika — [Parisian Gentleman](https://www.parisiangentleman.com/blog/q-a-with-pg-editors-should-i-buy-a-suit-from-a-bangkok-tailor)
- Thai traditional and occasion dress (Thai-language sources):
  - A provincial dressmaker (ห้องเสื้อ, Suphan Buri) making formal wear, silk, chut Thai and wedding gowns charges from 1,800 THB and earns about 30,000 THB a month (MGR Online SMEs, Oct 2023) — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/smes/detail/9660000088939)
  - Another shop prices fabric plus sewing plus hand embroidery at 1,300–4,900 THB, and ready-made Thai-fabric outfits sell for 490–1,970 THB (search snippet) — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/local/detail/9660000007988); [Wongnai Thai dress shops](https://www.wongnai.com/beauty-tips/thai-dress-shop)
- School uniforms and alterations:
  - Letting out a waist or lengthening a leg costs 60–100 THB (Chiang Mai, not Bangkok) — [Chiang Mai CityLife](https://www.chiangmaicitylife.com/citynews/general/cost-of-school-uniforms-lead-to-long-tailoring-queues/)
  - A custom-made uniform item costs 800–1,500 THB and takes 10 days (international school uniform notice, OLD 2012–2016) — [ISB parent portal](https://inside.isb.ac.th/parentportal/2015/07/27/uniform-information-for-2015-2016-school-year)
  - Parents in Yala had old uniforms altered to save money — [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/things-are-expensive-in-yala-parents-are-having-old-school-uniforms-altered-instead-of-buying-new-ones-to-save-money-during-the-start-of-the-s)

### Inferences
- In THB, a tourist-belt suit (6,000–10,000) costs about 3–5 times a full chut Thai from a local dressmaker, and about 100 times a basic alteration. The app's pricing and positioning have to work for very different shop revenues. Rough per-order guesses: tourist shop orders of 20,000–50,000 THB (several garments each) against Thai-local shops at 1,000–5,000 THB per order.
- Turnaround runs from 8 hours to 4 weeks, so order tracking has to handle same-day fitting cycles as well as multi-week ones.

### Gaps
- I found no consolidated price list for corporate uniform orders or for women's dresses at tourist shops.
- I found no 2025–26 price data for luxury Thai ateliers (Thai couture houses for weddings and galas).

## 3. Customers per segment and the tourism effect

### Takeaway
The tourist bespoke segment depends on international arrivals, which fell 7.2% in 2025 to 32.9 million and were still down 3.8% for January–September 2026. Arrivals have shifted toward short-haul and Indian visitors. Thai-local tailors depend on domestic demand (weddings, uniforms, alterations), which held up better.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 international arrivals fell 7.23% to 32.9 million. This was the first annual decline in a decade outside the COVID years. International tourism revenue fell 4.71% to 1.53 trillion THB (US$48.8bn) — [Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/tourism/2025/12/11/thailand-tourist-arrivals-fall-to-32m-amid-challenging-2025/); [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/thailand-tourism-slips-in-2025-despite-domestic-growth/)
- Causes cited for the drop: kidnapping fears, the March 2025 earthquake, the Hat Yai floods, politics, tariffs and the Cambodia border dispute. By market, Chinese arrivals were down about 33% (projected), Hong Kong 29%, Korea 16% and Taiwan 11% — [Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/tourism/2025/12/11/thailand-tourist-arrivals-fall-to-32m-amid-challenging-2025/); [Skift](https://skift.com/?p=793456)
- Domestic tourism rose in 2025: 202.37 million Thai trips (+2.70%) generating 1.167 trillion THB (+3.69%) — [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/thailand-tourism-slips-in-2025-despite-domestic-growth/)
- January–September 2026: 23.2 million international arrivals (−3.8% year on year). Top markets were China 3.82M, Malaysia 2.89M, India 1.68M, Russia 1.25M and South Korea 0.83M. Short-haul markets are recovering while long-haul is in a seasonal slowdown — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40071711); [Intellinews (Jan–Aug −3.1%)](https://www.intellinews.com/thailand-foreign-tourist-arrivals-fall-3-1-in-first-eight-months-of-2026-466255/)
- Customers named by shops:
  - Sukhumvit shops advertise US Secret Service, DEA and embassy officials as clients (forum snippet) — [Superfuture forum](https://supertalk.superfuture.com/topic/120967-best-tailor-in-bangkok/)
  - Shops offer wedding outfits (Jhasper), hotel pickup and in-room fittings (Om Fashion at the Royal Orchid Sheraton, Tailored By Luis), and worldwide remote ordering (Woolrich, Perfect Tailor, Om Fashion) — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- Domestic dressmakers get custom orders from weddings and formal events through Facebook wedding-attire groups. Post-COVID custom demand fell, and young Thais lack the money for bespoke — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/smes/detail/9660000088939)

### Inferences
- With Chinese visitors down, tourist shops become more dependent on Western long-haul repeat visitors, Indian visitors (now the #3 market, with wedding and formal-wear demand), Russians, and expat or business clients. The value of a repeat or remote customer goes up, which supports the app's stored-measurement and remote-reorder features.
- Domestic demand held up better than foreign demand, which is an argument for Thai-language support for local shops.

### Gaps
- I found no breakdown of tailor customers by nationality. Visitor mix is used here as a proxy.
- I found no data on the corporate uniform order market.

## 4. How shops operate day to day

### Takeaway
Many tourist-belt shops are sales fronts owned by Indian-Thai (often Sikh/Punjabi) families. Cutting and sewing is outsourced to workshops, and several fittings are squeezed into 1–3 days. Shops keep measurements "on file" to sell remote reorders and ship abroad. They talk to clients over WhatsApp, LINE and Facebook. I found no direct evidence of the actual recording tools (paper, Excel and so on).

### Cited Findings
- "Most of the shops are sales fronts where no actual stitching happens on-site" (forum, via snippet) — [Superfuture forum](https://supertalk.superfuture.com/topic/120967-best-tailor-in-bangkok/)
- By contrast, Tailor on Ten is expat-founded and advertises in-house production, appointment-only service and remote reorders for regulars — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- Family businesses:
  - Jesse & Son: family-run for 30+ years
  - Tailored By Luis: "since 2001, now in its third generation", sends WhatsApp updates and ships internationally
  - Jhasper: 17 years, tracks client preferences for repeat orders
  - Michael Tailor: 20+ years
  — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- Narry is a Sikh family bespoke tailoring business in Bangkok with 35+ years, and family members work in the business (documentary listing) — [SikhLens: Narry the Tailor](https://films.sikhlens.com/films/narry-the-tailor)
- Woolrich, Om Fashion and Perfect Tailor keep measurements on file or archived so customers abroad can reorder. Woolrich gives a 1-year warranty and free alterations — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- Tourist shops often claim one fitting is enough. Customers report needing at least three — [Fodor's / forum summary](https://www.fodors.com/community/asia/tailor-how-much-time-517262)
- The tuk-tuk commission model: drivers get gas vouchers or commission for bringing tourists to tailor shops — [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/all-tuk-tuks-lead-to-the-tailor-shop/); [Wikipedia: Thai tailor scam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_tailor_scam)
- A Thai-local dressmaker example: single-person handmade production, about 30,000 THB a month with unpredictable revenue, customers from word of mouth and Facebook groups — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/smes/detail/9660000088939)

### Inferences
- In the sales-front model the shop has to pass measurements and order specs to an outside workshop and track work-in-progress across several garments per tourist under tight deadlines. Workshop hand-off and order status are core needs.
- Deposits are standard practice in the trade (typically about 50% at order for tourist shops), but I found no source in this research to confirm it. See Gaps.

### Gaps
- I found no source describing how measurements are physically recorded (paper order books, Excel, LINE photos). This needs primary interviews.
- I found no source on deposit percentages or payment methods (PromptPay QR, cards).
- I found no source on Nepali-run tailor shops in Bangkok. Searches returned only Sikh/Punjabi sources.
- I could not fetch profiles of Rajawongse, Raja's, Narry, Pinky, Dynasty, Marco or Empire (forum pages blocked).

## 5. Pain points and complaints

### Takeaway
The best-documented problems are quality inconsistency and mislabelled fabric, tout and tuk-tuk scams, too few fittings, and the resulting damage to trust in the trade. Operational problems (lost measurements, late delivery, staff turnover, fabric stock) are plausible but poorly documented online.

### Cited Findings
- The "Thai tailor scam" is described as one of the most common confidence tricks in Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket and Khao Lak. A friendly stranger promotes cheap tailoring from a supposed newly opened export company — [Wikipedia: Thai tailor scam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_tailor_scam)
- Tuk-tuk detours to tailor shops for commission are still listed in 2025–26 scam guides — [Thailand Insider Guide](https://thailandinsiderguide.com/?p=4522); [PJ Media, Jul 2025](https://pjmedia.com/benbartee/2025/07/27/samsung-and-i-paint-the-town-red-the-tuk-tuk-hustle-n4942132)
- Fabric fraud and low quality (2015, OLD):
  - Synthetic blends sold as wool-cashmere, and fake designer fabric labels (D&G, LV)
  - Staff who cannot explain canvassing
  - Bundle deals like "$700 for three suits, two pants, five shirts" called unrealistic
  - Many TripAdvisor-top shops described as "glorified tourist traps"
  — [Parisian Gentleman](https://www.parisiangentleman.com/blog/q-a-with-pg-editors-should-i-buy-a-suit-from-a-bangkok-tailor)
- Specific defects reported, such as trousers delivered without the back pocket — [StyleForum](https://www.styleforum.net/threads/thai-tailor.51058/)
- Thai-local dressmakers' problems: falling custom demand against cheap ready-to-wear and imports, unpredictable income, and a handmade model that cannot scale — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/smes/detail/9660000088939)

### Inferences
- Reputation and trust are the main competitive currency. Features that make a shop look professional (branded receipts with deposit records, fitting schedules, photo records of the agreed fabric) could serve as trust signals against the "scam" image.
- Recording the fabric chosen, with a photo and swatch ID, protects both sides in fabric-substitution disputes.

### Gaps
- I found no systematic review analysis (TripAdvisor or Google review mining) quantifying complaint types.
- I found no evidence on staff turnover or the shortage of skilled tailors in Bangkok.

## 6. Market trends: digital, social, government programmes, PDPA

### Takeaway
LINE (56 million Thai users) and Facebook dominate how Thai SMEs sell and talk to customers. Remote reorder by stored measurements is now a standard marketing claim among Bangkok tailors. PDPA enforcement stepped up in 2025, with total fines passing 21 million THB, and it applies whatever the business size.

### Cited Findings
- LINE Thailand has 56 million users (85.4% of Thai internet users, 2025). There were more than 12 billion LINE Official Account conversations in 2025, about 6 million LINE OAs used by businesses and agencies, and about 800 million new OA friend-adds in H1 2026 — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/digital-marketing-en/line-thailand-business-solutions-2026/)
- LINE raised monthly OA fees for Thai SMEs, which drew criticism (date not confirmed) — [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/messaging-app-line-lambasted-for-raising-monthly-fees-for-thai-smes)
- Facebook is the most-used platform for Thai MSMEs to promote, sell and talk to clients, followed by LINE — [Statista (snippet)](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1424537/thailand-leading-online-platforms-among-msmes)
- Go Digital ASEAN trained 44,000+ Thai individuals and MSMEs for free, and 69% reported revenue growth — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/40047754)
- OSMEP sought a 1 billion THB budget to boost SME competitiveness — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/blogs/business/trade/40044166)
- Remote ordering, measurements on file and international shipping are advertised by Woolrich, Om Fashion, Perfect Tailor, Tailored By Luis and Tailor on Ten (2026) — [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/shopping/the-best-tailor-shops-in-bangkok)
- PDPA enforcement:
  - On 1 Aug 2025 the PDPC announced 8 administrative fines across 5 cases totalling 14.5 million THB, bringing total fines since enforcement began past 21 million THB
  - A tech retailer was fined 7 million THB for no security measures, no DPO and an unreported breach
  - A cosmetics company was fined 2.5 million THB
  - An online reservation-system breach of 200,000 records led to fines of 500,000 THB (toy company) plus 3 million THB (its data processor)
  - Commentators say enforcement applies "regardless of organizational type or size"
  — [DLA Piper Privacy Matters](https://privacymatters.dlapiper.com/2025/09/thailand-pdpa-crackdown-2025-are-you-next-major-fines-and-lessons-from-thailands-latest-enforcement/); [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/more-than-a-warning-eight-serious-fines-imposed-in-thai-data-protection-cases/)

### Inferences
- In the reservation-system case the data processor was fined more than the controller. A SaaS app holding tailors' customer data (names, phones, body measurements, photos) is a data processor under PDPA and carries its own liability. It will need a data processing agreement, security measures and breach-notification procedures. Body measurements and photos are personal data. They are probably not "sensitive" data under PDPA section 26 unless they reveal health information, but this needs legal confirmation.
- Shops already talk to clients on LINE. A LINE share/notify integration, or at least easy LINE-link sharing of receipts and reminders, fits how Thai customers expect to be contacted. Foreign repeat clients use WhatsApp.

### Gaps
- I found no statistic on smartphone ownership among Thai small business owners specifically.
- I found no government programme aimed specifically at tailors or garment micro-enterprises (e.g. DEPA vouchers) in this research.

## 7. Market size: garment/tailoring, custom clothing, SME counts

### Takeaway
No published market size exists for Thai custom tailoring. The context figures are: Thailand's textile market about US$4.9bn (2025), apparel exports of about US$6.0bn (2023), 3.26 million SMEs (99.5% of enterprises), and 32.9 million foreign tourists spending 1.53 trillion THB (2025).

### Cited Findings
- Thailand's textile market is estimated at US$4.91bn in 2025, rising to US$4.97bn in 2026 and US$5.37bn by 2031 (1.56% CAGR). Fashion and apparel make up 62.67% of it (Mordor Intelligence via GII) — [GII Research](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/moi2114409-thailand-textile-market-share-analysis-industry.html)
- Thai garment and textile exports were US$6.033bn in 2023, with the US the largest market at 18.65%. Apparel exports rose 6.28% in January–August 2025, and T-shirts overtook innerwear as the top category — [Fibre2Fashion](https://www.fibre2fashion.com/news/apparel-news/thailand-s-apparel-exports-rise-6-28-in-jan-aug-2025-306384-newsdetails.htm); [AlchemPro](https://www.alchempro.com/news/apparel-news/thailand-apparel-exports-rise-in-2025-as-t-shirts-overtake-innerwear-307435-newsdetails.htm)
- In 2024 there were 3,255,957 SME operators (99.5% of all enterprises, +0.9% year on year), employing over 13.4 million people, nearly 70% of employment (OSMEP) — [Thairath English](https://en.thairath.co.th/news/politic/2906013); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/blogs/business/trade/40044166)
- An example of single-shop revenue: a provincial dressmaker making about 30,000 THB (about US$900) a month — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/smes/detail/9660000088939)

### Inferences
- A rough illustrative estimate, NOT sourced: if a few hundred tourist-belt shops each sell a few hundred suits a year at 8,000–15,000 THB, tourist bespoke in Bangkok would be in the low billions of THB a year. Only report it as a hypothesis.

### Gaps
- No custom/made-to-measure clothing market size for Thailand or Southeast Asia was found from a credible source.
- No NSO or DBD count of tailoring establishments (TSIC 1410 / 9529) was retrieved.
- No count of SMEs in the apparel retail and services subsector specifically.
